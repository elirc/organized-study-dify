# Two-Week Cram Plan

Assumes ~3 focused hours/weekday, more on weekends. Every "aloud" item means actually speaking — the interview is an oral exam.

## Week 1 — load the evidence

**Day 1**: [00-fast-track](../00-fast-track.md) install + Flow 1 trace. Aloud: 3-minute chat-flow teach-back.
**Day 2**: Flows 2–3 ([01/05-key-flows](../01-codebase-cartography/05-key-flows.md)). Cards: 01-js-ts Q1–Q5 aloud.
**Day 3**: Flows 4–6. Cards: 03-api Q3–Q5 (validation, authz, IDOR) — these anchor to the flows you just read.
**Day 4**: [03/05-pattern-catalog](../03-architecture-and-patterns/05-pattern-catalog.md) patterns 1–7. Cards: 01-js Q6–Q10. One annotation drill ([04/01](../04-code-reading-gym/01-annotation-drills.md) — drill 1 or 5).
**Day 5**: patterns 8–14 + [03/06-architecture-critique](../03-architecture-and-patterns/06-architecture-critique.md). Cards: 02-frontend Q1–Q6.
**Day 6 (weekend)**: **Mock system design #1** — full [04-system-design walkthrough](04-system-design-from-this-repo.md) recorded, 45 min. Review recording against the grading section. Then STAR stories 1–2 drafted.
**Day 7**: **Checkpoint**. Self-assess: Can you (a) trace all 6 flows from memory at whiteboard level, (b) answer 01-js Q1/Q4/Q5 + 03-api Q4/Q5 at the *mid* bar, (c) tell story 1 in <2 min? Whatever failed = Day 8 morning's first hour.

## Week 2 — pressure and polish

**Day 8**: patch checkpoint gaps. Cards: 03-api Q7–Q12. STAR stories 3–4.
**Day 9**: **Timed debugging round #1** — sim D1 ([05-debugging rounds](05-debugging-and-code-review-rounds.md)), 20 min recorded. Cards: 02-frontend Q7–Q12.
**Day 10**: **Review sim R1** aloud (25 min) + re-read [07/01 review mindset](../07-career-and-collaboration/01-code-review-mindset.md). Remaining 01-js cards (Q11–Q14).
**Day 11**: **Timed debugging round #2** — sim D2 + D3 back-to-back (35 min). STAR stories 5–7 (or adapt 1–4 deeper if you haven't shipped tickets).
**Day 12**: **Mock system design #2** — one *variation* prompt from 04 (pick "10× traffic" or "real-time collab"), 30 min recorded. Review sims R2+R3 rapid-fire.
**Day 13 (weekend)**: full dress rehearsal: 10 shuffled cards aloud (mixed decks) + one 2-min flow teach-back + three STAR stories + 15-min mini-design. Ruthless recording review; write your three weakest answers out longhand.
**Day 14**: **Checkpoint 2 + taper.** Re-run only the three weakest items. Prepare your own questions for interviewers (two per round type). Sleep.

## Daily constants (15 min)

- Two flash-cards re-answered from yesterday (spaced repetition beats cramming).
- One anchor re-opened in the actual code — keep the muscle memory of *pointing at evidence*.

## The night before

Re-read only: your STAR story sheet, the system-design Step 6 tradeoffs recap, and the golden rule (example + tradeoff + failure mode). Nothing new.
